# Study Content Review

Reviewed: October 8, 2026.

## Scope

This update reviews the seven application units' key points and study notes, not
every answer in the 1,438-question bank. It does not claim that every textbook has
been reread or that any particular question is guaranteed to appear on the exam.
Question IDs, answer keys, stored progress, authentication and AI settings are
unchanged. `study-guides.js` is the shared source for the reviewed sidebar notes
and expanded unit guides.

## Exam Scope

The [official exam description](https://www.nsca.com/certification/nsca-cpt/nsca-cpt-exam-description/)
links to the [2025 JTA and detailed content outline](https://www.nsca.com/globalassets/certification/certification-pdfs/nsca-certified-personal-trainer-job-task-analysis-summary-2025).
The four scored domains are consultation/assessment (23%), planning (29%),
execution (36%), and safety/emergency/legal issues (12%). The app's seven units
are learning categories, not official exam domains or a prediction of item frequency.

The PDF heading gives an effective date of July 1, 2025, while one bullet
inconsistently says July 1, 2024 and refers to pages 2-14. This is an apparent
source typo; the app links to the document rather than silently rewriting it.

ECG waveform interpretation is not explicitly listed as a separate task. It was
moved from core sidebar memorization to supplementary context. This is a study
priority judgment, not a claim that basic cardiac physiology cannot appear.

## Changes by Unit

| Unit | Review outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Exercise science | Explain body-relative planes, axes, muscle roles, overlapping energy systems and torque; demote detailed ECG memorization. |
| Assessment | Distinguish reliability from validity; qualify jump, flexibility, change-of-direction and 1RM testing. |
| Technique/safety | Explain phased movement, spotting, individual technique, breathing cautions and useful coaching feedback. |
| Program design | Distinguish relative intensity, volume-load and physical work; cover FITT, progression and special-population constraints. |
| Nutrition | Explain nutrient functions; include sleep, stress and RED-S; avoid guarantees about supplements reducing soreness. |
| Facility safety | Remove unsupported universal spacing minima; emphasize applicable standards, emergency roles, documentation and privacy. |
| Video practice | Treat visual questions as a cross-domain format; prioritize joint-specific observation and concrete corrections. |

## User-Supplied Illustrations

Three originals are preserved in `assets/user-guides/`. Their provenance beyond
the user's submission is not established. They are teaching illustrations, not
validated measurements. Corrections appear before the images and in zoom captions.

- Moment arm: the perpendicular distance to the force line is not limb length.
  In a standing curl with upper arm vertical, the external gravitational moment
  is usually greatest with the forearm horizontal, not simply with the elbow straight.
- Planes: camera viewpoints are mnemonics, not definitions. Body-relative planes
  rotate with posture; knee flexion illustrates why flexion is not always forward.
- Bench press: the illustrated curve is not experimental data. Shoulder and elbow
  moments require separate analysis; grip and bar path matter. Sticking during
  ascent is not explained by a universal increase in moment arm during descent.

Illustrations are matched conservatively to question/option terminology. They
do not replace or silently rewrite question answer keys.
